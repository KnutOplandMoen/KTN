Kurose & Ross · 8th edition (2021)

Course curriculum

Overview of which parts of the textbook and supplementary material are included in TTM4100 — Communication: Services and Networks. The text is based on the official curriculum list for the course.

Textbook

Book outline

The curriculum follows mainly the eighth global edition of Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach (Pearson, 2021).

  • Intro: Computer networks and the internet
  • Application layer
  • Transport layer
  • Network layer
  • Link layer
  • Wireless and mobile
Chapters 1–4

Foundation layers

Chapter 1 — the entire chapter is curriculum.

Chapter 2 — the entire chapter is curriculum, except:

  • Part of section 2.5 on BitTorrent: pages 170–173

Chapter 3 — the entire chapter is curriculum, except:

  • 3.6.1 The Causes and the Costs of Congestion
  • 3.7.2 Network-Assisted Explicit Congestion Notification (ECN) and Delay-based Congestion Control

Chapter 4 — the entire chapter is curriculum, except:

  • 4.4 Generalized Forwarding and SDN
  • 4.6 Middleboxes
Chapter 5

Network layer — selected sections

From chapter 5, only the following is curriculum:

  • 5.1 Introduction
  • 5.2 Routing algorithms — introduction only (not 5.2.1 or 5.2.2)
  • 5.6 ICMP: The Internet Control Message Protocol
Chapters 6–7

Link layer and wireless

Chapter 6 — the entire chapter is curriculum, except:

  • 6.4.4 VLANs
  • 6.5 Link Virtualization
  • 6.6 Data Center Networking

Chapter 7 — the entire chapter is curriculum, except:

  • 7.2.1 CDMA
  • 7.3.6 Bluetooth
  • 7.5 Mobility Management: Principles
  • 7.6 Mobility Management in Practice
  • 7.7 Wireless and Mobility: Impact on Higher-Layer Protocols
Lectures and exercises

What the curriculum covers

The curriculum matches what is taught in lectures and includes:

  1. Slide sets
  2. Reference material provided in lectures (if any)
  3. Large parts of J. F. Kurose and K. W. Ross, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach (8th global edition, 2021), Pearson Higher Education
  4. A copy of (parts of) chapter 9 from the 7th edition of the same textbook (not included in the 8th edition). Can be downloaded from the course Blackboard site.

Item (3): See the lists above and the book’s table of contents for details on what is curriculum from the 8th edition.

The content of all exercises is also part of the course curriculum.

Chapter 8

Security in computer networks

From chapter 8, only the following is curriculum:

  • 8.1 What Is Network Security?
  • 8.2 Principles of Cryptography — without a detailed treatment of RSA (pp. 650–654)
  • 8.3 Message Integrity and Digital Signatures
  • 8.5 (introduction) and 8.5.1 Secure E-mail
  • 8.6 Securing TCP Connections: TLS
  • 8.7 (introduction) and 8.7.1 IPSec and VPNs
  • 8.8 Securing Wireless LANs and 4G/5G Cellular Networks
  • 8.9 (introduction) and 8.9.1 Firewalls

See also chapter 8 on this site: Chapter 8 — Security.

  • 9.2 Streaming Stored Video
  • 9.3 Voice-over-IP
  • 9.4 Protocols for Real-Time Conversational Applications
  • 9.4.1 RTP — not 9.4.2 SIP
